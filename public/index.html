    Sync buffer

    Lead time before a scheduled start. Longer absorbs slow Wi-Fi; shorter feels snappier.

    Each device runs an NTP-style exchange, estimates its own clock drift, then schedules the exact sample that lands on a shared instant. Residual error is pulled back by micro-resampling.
